# CS230_SDD
Software Design Document for The Gaming Room

1.Briefly summarize The Gaming Room client and their software requirements. Who was the client? What type of software did they want you to design?

The Gaming Room is a company which designs games for the modern world. Their latest project was Draw It Or Lose It, which was an application that strictly ran on the Android OS. The Gaming Room wanted to make DILI into a game which ran on a web browser, so any platform or OS was capable of running it. 

2. What did you do particularly well in developing this documentation?

I did a great job in the explanation of Executive Summary, Requirements, Design Constraints, and the various other sections.

3. What about the process of working through a design document did you find helpful when developing the code?

The UML diagram is probably the most useful, as it allows you to get a better idea of what the actual Class, method, and attribute requirements structurally will be, before ever writing a single line. It gives you guideposts, a place to start, and doesn't leave you stressing over where to start.

4.If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

I think my recommendations section was a little lacking. I think next time I would do more research on architectures, to better understand the usage.,

5. How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your software design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?

I interpreted the users needs well, and understood their objective, their design constraints, and what they were looking to accomplish with the software. With that, that is the importance. If you are not designing something for yourself, then you need to follow what the customer is looking for, even if they don't know what's best, and work within their constraints. Make sure you are aiming for the target they have set, rather than creating something wildly different.

6. How did you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a similar software application?

I heavily used the information that the client had provided when designing the software. I wanted to ensure I was following their intended outcome and design. I created the design document, and would certainly use it again in the future for good structure, which helps to keep you on track and focused. Its always good to work up some prototypes after the design document is completed, and get some feedback from the client, to see what is working from the document, and what could use improvement.
